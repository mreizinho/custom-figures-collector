Avoid:
painterly rendering
CGI appearance
artificial textures
over-sharpening
excessive smoothing
halos around edges
Consistency
Every image in this project should follow the same visual standard.
The collection should appear as though every miniature was photographed:
with the same camera
using the same lens
under identical studio lighting
against the same white seamless background
at the same scale
from the same viewing angle
Guiding Principle
Treat every uploaded image as a photograph of a unique physical collectible.
Your task is to produce a cleaner, standardized catalogue photograph of that exact
photographed object.
